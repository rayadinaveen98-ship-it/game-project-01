# GP-01 | Comparative production and business feasibility - discovery stage
The following **content counts** are planning exercises, not measured workload, user demand, price or revenue.

| Dimension | P - Coupled resource | D - Contradiction | M - Montage interpretation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Reusable engine logic | Switch and target solver; puzzle-state recompute | Evidence card state; rule validation; case graph | Sequence timeline; scene outcome observation |
| First paper test units | 2 fully defined boards | 3 compact cases | 3 storyboard triptychs (9 cards) |
| Hypothetical tiny digital slice | 8-12 verified boards | 2-3 authored caselets | 3-4 illustrated montage challenges |
| Hard-to-scale content | Novel puzzle mechanics and variety | Fair story/evidence writing and translation | Original legible art and reactions for each shot |
| Candidate visual language | Clean tokens and high-contrast vector controls | Editorial evidence cards / understated noir | Abstract, readable cinematic storyboards |
| Distinctiveness risk | Resembles resource-routing logic games | Resembles existing detective reconstruction games | Ordering frames exists; interpretation is subjective |
| Possible commercial route | Ads only if repeat sessions justify; optional hint rewarded ads | Free case/demo + one-time unlock | Premium short experience or free demo + unlock |
| Key unknown | Whether coupled allocations are fun | Whether deductions feel fair and satisfying | Whether audience reactions depend on player decisions |

**No platform or business model is approved.** The studio-tycoon progression framework is explicitly NOT included in the core M experiment until shot-order play is validated; adding a budget layer now would contaminate the test.

## Proposed visual-readability gates
- All important meaning must be visible without audio or color alone; pair Sun/Rain with text or icon.
- Evidence text must survive at common mobile viewport sizes; no tiny newspaper-print UI.
- Storyboard iconography must be recognizable to non-filmmakers. Ambiguity is measurable; don't over-polish away gameplay uncertainty before testing.
