# D | Evidence contradiction: three fair cases
**Format:** 3 statements, 3-4 evidence cards and one immutable world rule. Players must link an exact statement and enough evidence to disprove it. Cases are independent.

## D1 - Studio Door (beginner)
**Rule:** Electronic door log captures every opening with exact, synchronized timestamps; it cannot miss one.
**Statements**
- S1 / Rey: "The studio door did not open after 19:10."
- S2 / Mira: "I left my notebook at the desk at 19:00."
- S3 / Dev: "I was in the cafe by 19:15."
**Objective evidence**
- E1: 19:00 desk inventory lists Mira's notebook.
- E2: 19:14 studio door OPEN (signed event log).
- E3: 19:15 studio door CLOSED (same log).
- E4: 19:13 cafe purchase recorded using Dev's card.
**Facilitator:** Link S1 + E2 + rule. E2 definitively refutes S1. E4 does not establish that Dev was in cafe but also does not refute his claim. Other statements are compatible with the logs.

## D2 - The Staircase (intermediate)
**Rule:** The ONLY usable path between floor 1 scan point and floor 3 scan point requires at least three minutes. Scan events log **card ID**, not person; credentials can be copied. Clocks are synchronized and exact.
**Statements**
- S1 / Ari: "I personally tapped my card at the floor 1 reader at 09:04 AND at floor 3 at 09:05."
- S2 / Bea: "Someone could have copied Ari's ID."
- S3 / Guard: "The lift was shut down all morning."
**Evidence**
- E1: 09:04 floor 1 scanner, Ari's card ID.
- E2: 09:05 floor 3 scanner, Ari's card ID.
- E3: Lift service record: out of service 09:00-09:30.
- E4: Badge desk policy: credentials may be copied to another device (no biometrics).
**Facilitator:** Link S1 + E1 + E2 + rule. Two people can use devices bearing the same ID; Ari cannot personally make both taps with <3 minutes between. This puzzle tests causal logic, not deduction of guilt. S2 and S3 remain compatible.

## D3 - Empty Seat (applied counting)
**Rule:** The venue has exactly one gate; each attendee must scan their unique ticket exactly once on entry; the scanner never misses an entry. Ticket purchase does not itself imply attendance.
**Statements**
- S1 / Host: "Everyone who bought a ticket entered the venue."
- S2 / Clerk: "At least one customer might still be outside."
- S3 / Usher: "There were no staff or complimentary entries."
**Evidence**
- E1: Final sales sheet: 50 unique tickets sold.
- E2: Entrance scanner tally: 49 unique tickets scanned at the end of entry.
- E3: No complimentary tickets were issued.
- E4: Venue closed entry, 19:30.
**Facilitator:** Link S1 + E1 + E2 + rule; at least one ticket buyer did not enter. S2 is possible, S3 consistent. Do not claim to know which buyer stayed outside.

## Authoring quality standard
Every falsifiable claim must be backed by an explicit world rule. Alternate explanations should be examined. For user tests, do not identify the truthful witness or show answers until responses are recorded. Measure actual time to discover a rule-based contradiction and how many irrelevant cards are consulted.
