# GP-01 — Research recommendations and decision gate (not concept selection)
**As of:** 2026-11-07

## What the research supports
- **Puzzle:** Mechanic readability and reusable art are strong production fits. But energy rotation/arrow removal are existing saturated designs. A **new systemic tension** needs testing, and an ad-led free model cannot be assumed to generate meaningful income without distribution and returning players.
- **Mystery:** A pure deduction interaction can align with narrative quality. Free trial + one-time unlock is an existing model, though content/writing and purchase conversion risks remain unresolved.
- **Tycoon:** Audience exists for creator/management fantasies and paid games. **Film-specific studio tycoon is not a gap**. First-release systems depth and content feasibility are greatest concerns.

## Best next comparative experiment *subjects*, not selected finalists
- **P:** Multi-constraint spatial resource routing (requires novelty test).
- **D:** Detect a provable contradiction from mobile-sized evidence cards.
- **M:** Choose shot order and observe a change in audience interpretation, with budget constraint only if the core verb works.

These subjects have distinct player verbs and can be compared in low-fidelity form. They are not product choices; they may all fail.

## Commercial decision is postponed
Potential models to test: (1) free with optional rewarded hints/skins (if gameplay replayable and no exploitative pressure), (2) free chapter/demo + one-time unlock (if finite authored content), (3) upfront premium (if strong distinct artistic value), (4) fair cosmetic monetization (only if suitable). Platform, price, age rating, data collection, distribution channels and budget remain **OPEN**.

## Decision gate before a game name or concept is locked
- [ ] At least 3 micro-concepts illustrated with *original* rule diagrams, no borrowed artwork.
- [ ] 5-second comprehension protocol executed with real first-time viewers.
- [ ] Basic replay/fun tests executed and logged; failure reasons retained.
- [ ] Distinctiveness checked against direct current mobile comparators, not only genre leaders.
- [ ] Evidence-based scores entered; no invented metrics.
- [ ] Estimate content/asset units per planned first release.
- [ ] Check install/monetization strategy with concrete audience and distribution plan.
- [ ] User explicitly approves a concept **after** results.

## Provenance boundaries
This pack records public information gathered in a browsing session and assistant interpretations. It does not imply we downloaded or played competitors, interviewed users, secured rights to any assets, proven any APK build path, or conducted a legal IP search.
