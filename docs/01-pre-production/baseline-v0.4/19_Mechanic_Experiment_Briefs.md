# GP-01 — Differentiation and falsification experiments
**Version:** 0.3 · **Status:** PROPOSED ONLY. Concepts unchosen, no designs prototyped, no user tests run.

## Hypothesis P: multi-constraint nature puzzle (not named Lumen Grove)
**Player verb:** rotate, redirect and balance **two conflicting resource flows** (e.g. light and water) through one tiny board, achieving environmental goals without starving neighbors.
- Why investigate: energy pipe rotation exists in [Energy: Anti-Stress Loops](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.infinitygames.loopenergy), so *two interdependent systems with tradeoffs* must be the novel consequence, not the graphics.
- 5-second promise: 'Every connection feeds one plant and can deprive another.'
- Mechanic-only experiment: 6 hand-sketched boards, one resource only (control) vs two resource types (test), no garden illustrations.
- Kill conditions: players perceive the new rule as confusing rather than satisfying; same solution as ordinary wire rotation; no understandable undo state.
- Scope: 6 simple boards, reversible actions, proof of solvability; **no claim** of originality or visual style approval.

## Hypothesis D: contradiction-based micro-investigation
**Player verb:** compare 3 short statements and 4 timestamped observations; choose a **provable contradiction** and explain it by linking exact evidence.
- Why investigate: [Golden Idol](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.playstack.TheCaseOfTheGoldenIdol) validates deduction fantasy; [Duskwood](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.everbytestudio.interactive.text.chat.story.rpg.cyoa.duskwood) reveals a complaint hypothesis around extraneous minigames.
- 5-second promise: 'Find what cannot both be true.'
- Test: 3 self-contained cases, every solution demonstrably unique, all clues accessible without audio, clear evidence retrieval. Measure time to first valid link and false-positive reasoning.
- Kill: players must guess rather than reason; text too small on device; content takes excessive time to author; cases feel like trick questions.
- Scope: no branching megastory, no filmed actors, no chat simulation, no paywall.

## Hypothesis M: film editing as the actual management decision
**Player verb:** reassemble **three short abstract shots** to create a chosen emotional effect, then face budget/audience trade-offs. This is not just selecting film genres and casting actors.
- Why investigate: [Silver Screen Story](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=net.kairosoft.android.pictures), [Movie Tycoon](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=dev.blacksnow.movietycoon) and [Call The Shots](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.catmacho.calltheshots) already cover film/crew/finance simulation broadly.
- 5-second promise: 'Your editing choices change how the audience reads the scene.'
- Test: 4 sets of original stylized storyboard panels; allow a three-shot ordering and measure whether players can predict and interpret consequences. A budget restriction may be introduced later.
- Kill: outcomes feel arbitrary/scripted; it turns into a one-answer quiz; artwork volume or narrative logic is unaffordable; mobile drag interaction too fiddly.
- Scope: abstract original panels, no films/franchises, no cinematics; **not** a prototype commitment.

## Fair comparison plan
| Phase | Minimum proposed protocol | Evidence needed |
|---|---|---|
| Concept comprehension | 8–12 fresh viewers per hypothesis; 5-second explanation, no coaching | Raw anonymous answers, central verb understood by ≥2/3 (directional threshold) |
| Paper interaction | 5–8 fresh testers per hypothesis, matched 5–8 min sessions | First correct move, voluntary 2nd attempt, frustration, failure explanations |
| Content feasibility | Produce 3–6 'levels' with repeatable templates | Minutes per unit; whether rules allow content at realistic pace |
| Commercial concept probe | Ask preferences after neutral gameplay, not before | Reasons to return and willingness to pay / accept *optional* ads, NOT revenue forecast |

**Fairness / bias control:** Equal-quality instructions and testing time for each. Record failed tests too. The paper tests can be done without choosing an engine, production art or Android platform.
