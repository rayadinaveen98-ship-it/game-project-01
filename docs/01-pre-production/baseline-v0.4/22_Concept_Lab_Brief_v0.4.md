# GP-01 | Concept Lab - test package, not concept approval
**Version:** 0.4.0 | **Date:** 2026-11-07 | **Stage:** pre-production / research

## Objective
Compare three equally constrained **player actions**, not finished genres or art quality: 
- **P**: Flip a coupled valve to redistribute BOTH sunlight and water, reaching exact plant targets.
- **D**: Identify a demonstrable contradiction by linking a statement to the smallest sufficient set of objective evidence.
- **M**: Sequence three original shot cards to suggest an emotion; have a separate, blind viewer describe what the edit conveys.

## Deliverables produced
- Printable nine-page player/facilitator workbook (`31_Concept_Lab_Printable_v0.4.pdf`) and separate three-page pitch-stimulus deck (`32_Five_Second_Stimuli_v0.4.pdf`).
- Scenario definitions and solver evidence (P1: 8 configurations, P2: 16).
- Three textual mystery cases with explicit rules and non-spoiler answers on facilitator page.
- Three original, minimal vector storyboard triptychs and an unprimed audience procedure.
- Facilitation script, empty capture form, criteria and risk log.

## Lab protocol / bias controls
1. Have 8-12 first-time viewers per hypothesis see only the corresponding dedicated stimulus page for **5 seconds**. Hide it. Ask unprompted: "What do you think you do?" and "What would you do first?" Do not use the game category in the invitation.
2. Ask 5-8 fresh players per hypothesis to perform a paper-play session. 6-8 minutes is the default; each receives similarly brief instructions and an action target.
3. Alternate game order for anyone exposed to multiple prototypes; preferably compare independent participants for clean comprehension results. Don't play audio, use game art, or hint at the answer.
4. For **M**, the person assembling a sequence must NOT be the person rating its emotional meaning. Randomize card order before each participant.
5. Record raw, anonymous observations and negative results. Never invent a participant, score, or revenue estimate.

## Pre-committed directional signals (not scientific claims)
- Comprehension: at least 8/12 can state the verb without being prompted; record denominator exactly.
- Action: at least 4/6 can make a legal first move without facilitator intervention.
- Interest: at least 3/6 voluntarily choose a further attempt, recorded honestly.
- P: at least 4/6 can explain how one flip affects two resources. Internal examples must have a valid solution; no invented uniqueness claim.
- D: at least 4/6 can cite a rule + evidence, not guess who is lying.
- M: the claimed emotion should emerge from genuinely **blind** descriptions; no objective success marking without inter-rater data.

These numbers are *screening thresholds*, not significance tests. Passing them does not establish market demand, commercial viability or originality. The product owner chooses whether to pursue, alter, or discard any direction after results.
