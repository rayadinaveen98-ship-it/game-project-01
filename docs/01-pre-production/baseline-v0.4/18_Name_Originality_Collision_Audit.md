## Why this matters
- Copyright generally protects expression, not abstract game ideas. Trademark / passing-off risks are separate and jurisdiction-dependent. Our concern here also includes consumer confusion and product differentiation, not merely lawsuits.
- Similarity screening is **not** a declaration of unlawful copying and does **not** grant clearance for an alternate title.
- Even where a game title can legally coexist, choosing a confusingly similar title is a marketing and search-discoverability risk.

## Policy until concept approval
All C01–C08 labels are **internal working identifiers**. No finalized name, logo, domain, store listing or brand claims. Conduct app-store, search-engine, domain/social and jurisdiction-specific trademark reviews (India + intended markets) **after** mechanic has been validated. For any actual launch, consult relevant expertise where needed.
