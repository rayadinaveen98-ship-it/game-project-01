# DEC-020 — First game prototype selection

**Date:** 2026-11-07  
**Owner:** Product owner  
**Status:** APPROVED  
**Scope:** Which concept receives the first playable prototype, **not** which game will be shipped.

## Decision
- Prioritize a playable prototype of **C08 Snapline Sprint**, working title.
- Keep **C09 young-girl adventure** preserved as an interesting future concept. It is deferred, not rejected.
- C02-R cinematic detective remains a concept, not the active build.
- Objective remains 50% creative quality and 50% commercial viability.
- Prototype must demonstrate design quality and technical clarity; no commercial release approval.

## Not approved
- Exact Snapline mechanics or permanent game title
- Art direction, art assets or approved mockups
- Engine, Android-native platform, cloud hosting or distribution
- Monetization, audience targeting, launch timeline or feature additions
- Claim of novelty or projected revenue

## Change control
Read current prototype plan under `docs/04-game-design/snapline/`. Any later choice requires a separate decision, review evidence, and appropriate owner approval.
