**Date:** 2026-11-07

## Owner input

After the P6.4 resilient-input browser build, the owner replied: "good bro continue". This is positive project feedback and authorization to proceed to the next scoped step, not measured physical-device performance or an external player-study finding.

## Decision

- Keep **all three owner-approved gameplay modes**, eight authored courses, core physics and Pulse Grid art unchanged.
- Build an independent offline **P6.5 facilitator lab** that embeds the existing P6.4 single-file browser game.
- Study A/B/C first-time-player comprehension and enjoyment using the **two shared courses**; the B-only and A/C-only signature content cannot be treated as equivalent head-to-head difficulty.
- Counterbalance all six A/B/C orders across actual sessions, avoid personal identifiers, record only intentionally provided observations and export locally as JSON/CSV.
- **No genuine player sessions or findings are available yet.** The empty results state is deliberate. Engineering QA does not stand in for human feedback.
- Hold observations in memory until explicitly exported; do not auto-collect analytics or upload to cloud storage.
- Treat this as prototype research infrastructure, not a new production game screen, native Android build or monetization authorization.

## Next evidence gate

The owner or collaborators recruit consenting, unfamiliar players (5–8 suggested exploratory target), record genuine observations, and review raw results. Real-phone touch feel and actual browser progress storage verification remain separate outstanding P6.4 gates.
