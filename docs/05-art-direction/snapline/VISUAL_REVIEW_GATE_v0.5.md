# Snapline Sprint — visual production gate v0.5

**Status:** PROPOSED. No visual style or approved screenshots exist.

## Design principle
Visual consistency is an explicit acceptance criterion, not optional polish. Mockups alone do not prove in-engine quality.

## Three potential directions (proposals only)
**V1 — Pulse Grid**: minimalist high-contrast luminous lines on a deep neutral background. Crisp geometric hazards. Restrained neon and sparse kinetic feedback.

**V2 — Ink Trail**: warm paper/pencil vocabulary; pleasing hand-drawn strokes, textured but not muddy surfaces, rounded character movement.

**V3 — Micro Runner**: cheerful miniature object/world language, simple dimensional shadows, vivid solid colors, more character identity and art workload.

## Approval package needed before final art
- For each direction, one gameplay screenshot with identical obstacle positions and HUD, plus home/result treatment overview.
- Visual measurements: 9:16 portrait framing, color tokens, typography, clear safe area, animation easing, collision marker representation.
- Consistent avatar, start/finish markers, obstacles, ink gauge, GO/RETRY controls and instructions.
- Accessibility checks: color-independent hazard shapes, contrast, reduced motion and optional mute.
- Prototype must use **same geometry and gameplay** across visual options for a fair review.
- Owner explicitly selects visual direction and requests amendments; no unilateral finalization.

## After approval
- Store palettes, geometry, font scale, icon stroke, motion durations, layer depths and SFX references in versioned art bible.
- Canonical assets stored with provenance/license.
- Compare in-game captured screenshots against approved visuals during each implementation milestone.
- Review gameplay legibility after glow, particles and animation are added.

**Nothing here constitutes approval for a final art style.**
