# Snapline Sprint P6.5 — Practical Uncoached Player Study

**Status:** Study kit prepared; **zero genuine participant observations gathered** as of creation.

## Goal

Does a first-time phone player understand, control and willingly replay each of Snapline Sprint's three owner-approved modes: A Plan & Run, B Snap & Redirect and C Live Drawing? We are **not** choosing a winner or discarding any mode: all three remain in the planned product.

## Suggested sample

Start with 5–8 *actual unfamiliar players* as exploratory feedback only, when the owner can recruit them and obtain permission. Do not create simulated personas or report a participant count until sessions happen.

## Session flow (for facilitator)

1. Ask the person whether they agree to the game test and non-identifying written observations. Do not record names, email addresses, photos, voices or contact details. If they decline, don't record the session.
2. Open the P6.5 Offline Playtest Lab. Check the permission box and start an anonymous session. The order rotates across all six A/B/C permutations, mitigating order effects.
3. Ask: **“What do you think you do here?”** before providing instructions. Only give hints on request; tally hints.
4. For the current mode, have them select A/B/C themselves, then play **Static Detour** and **Moving Hazard**. A/B/C have different physical workloads, so ask about clarity and willingness to retry rather than declaring fastest mode “best.”
5. Observe first action comprehension, wins out of two, hint count, spontaneous retry, and optionally ask for ratings from 1–5 on enjoyment, phone comfort and collision fairness. Ratings may be left blank.
6. Record a specific failure/friction observation and optional verbatim quote only with permission. Avoid speculation about personal identity or ability.
7. Continue with the next mode in the displayed order. The current game can be reset from the lab; progress in the lab frame is not persistent.
8. Mark overall preference only if the participant expresses one; otherwise select undecided. Save session as complete (all modes) or partial (some modes).
9. **Export JSON and CSV** before closing the tab. There is no upload, account, background collection or persistent note store. Review raw observations later.

## Interpretation

Review first-action clarity, hint demand, rejected moves, unfair collision complaints, thumb fatigue, enjoyment and voluntary retry for each mode. Record how many actual players contributed to every claim. A handful of convenience testers is not representative, and completion times across A/B/C are intrinsically different.

## Explicit non-goals

- No fabricated playtest data, synthetic players, “retention rates,” market proof or user quotes.
- No third-party analytics, age/sex/location profiling, network upload or cloud database.
- No new gameplay content, art redesign or forced mode elimination.
- P6.5 study readiness is **not** commercial release readiness or physical Android performance certification.

## Owner review gate

After collecting sessions, decide whether the next work should be tutorial simplification, touch controls, hazard fairness, difficulty balancing or no code change. New work must have evidence and continue to match the approved Pulse Grid art bible.
